The problem we solve

Programmes create evidence as they work. Evaluations arrive after the fact.

After a development programme launches, evaluations and donor reporting often depend on new M&E staff reconstructing what happened from scattered records, interviews and fading memories. MtO captures the evidence while the programme is still happening.

Without MtO
“The evaluator arrives at the end and starts asking what happened.”
Staff turnover→Scattered files→Interviews→Six-week reconstruction

The result is expensive, incomplete, and distant from the decisions, risks and outcomes as they actually unfolded.

→
With MtO
“The record is built while the work is happening.”
Weekly updates→AI extraction→Human approval→Living evidence archive

Teams paste what they already write. MtO extracts outcomes, risks, decisions and open questions in real time and keeps every claim connected to its source.

01

M&E stops being retrospective

Live today

An external evaluator should not spend most of the budget excavating a programme through interviews with people who have moved on. Keep the record as you go and that excavation disappears.

02

Nobody writes the final report

Record live · export next

The programme officer should not spend six weeks assembling a document from memory and half-lost files. When every week is captured, the report becomes a query, not a project.

03

Lessons recorded when they happen

Live today

The reason a team changed approach is obvious in the moment and easy to lose by the end of the quarter. Risks, errors and decision logic are recorded while they are still true.

04

The next programme starts informed

Needs the pilot first

What one team learned the hard way can reach the team doing it next, in the next district or country. Knowledge compounds instead of evaporating.

End state: An efficient, full-coverage reporting and learning system built as programme work unfolds.
