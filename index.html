01The problem we solve

Programmes create evidence as they work. Evaluations arrive after the fact.

By the time evaluation begins, the programme has already moved through changes, risks, decisions and results that are difficult to reconstruct from scattered files.

The solution

Capture the road to the outcome.

MotOc is being built to connect the evidence that matters across the programme, so the final outcome, the path behind it, and the lessons learned are already visible when evaluation begins.

Without MotOc
“The M&E evaluator arrives at the end and starts asking what happened.”
Staff turnover→Scattered files→Interviews→Partial memory

The result is expensive, incomplete, and distant from the decisions, risks and outcomes as they actually unfolded.

→
With MotOc
“The outcome pathway is built while the programme is happening.”
Programme documents→AI extraction→Human review→Connected outcome evidence

MotOc is being built to work from the documentation teams already produce and surface the evidence that matters for outcomes, including significant changes, risks, results, lessons and evidence gaps. Every approved claim remains connected to its source.

WHAT MotOc DOES

Build the programme record while the work happens.

MotOc turns routine updates into a reviewed evidence record that teams can use for reporting, evaluation and future programme design.

04How it works

Existing updates go in. Reviewed evidence comes out.

MotOc is designed as a SaaS evidence layer on top of the documentation teams already create. AI extracts candidate signals, people review them, and approved items become part of the programme record.

  1. 01

    Upload

    Field reports, updates, meeting notes, PDFs and shared folders.

  2. 02

    Extract

    AI proposes outcomes, decisions, risks, changes, lessons and evidence gaps.

  3. 03

    Review

    People approve, edit or reject each proposed item against the source.

  4. 04

    Connect

    Approved evidence links to outcomes, locations, people, dates and documents.

  5. 05

    Use

    Teams use the record for reporting, evaluation, handover and future design.

Designed for private, source-linked evidence capture.
