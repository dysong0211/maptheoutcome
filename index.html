01The problem we solve

Programmes create evidence as they work. Evaluations arrive after the fact.

By the time evaluation begins, the programme has already moved through changes, risks, decisions and results that are difficult to reconstruct from scattered files.

The solution

Capture the road to the outcome.

MtO is being built to connect the evidence that matters across the programme, so the final outcome, the path behind it, and the lessons learned are already visible when evaluation begins.

MtO is being built to capture the outcome-relevant pathway while the programme unfolds. It organizes evidence from the documentation teams already produce, links it to intended outcomes, and preserves the material changes, risks, results and lessons that help explain the final outcome. The goal is not to track every move. It is to make the full road to the outcome visible when evaluation begins.

Without MtO
“The M&E evaluator arrives at the end and starts asking what happened.”
Staff turnover→Scattered files→Interviews→Partial memory

The result is expensive, incomplete, and distant from the decisions, risks and outcomes as they actually unfolded.

→
With MtO
“The outcome pathway is built while the programme is happening.”
Programme documents→AI extraction→Human review→Connected outcome evidence

MtO is being built to work from the documentation teams already produce and surface the evidence that matters for outcomes, including significant changes, risks, results, lessons and evidence gaps. Every approved claim remains connected to its source.

02Evidence snapshot

The evidence: where programme records become thin.

A five-country MtO audit examines who is named in programme records. A deeper Cambodia inspection asks what results those records contain. Together, they raise a question: how much of a programme’s outcome story can be traced from its public record?

What this finding can tell us.

The preliminary figures suggest a gap in the information published in the inspected records. They do not measure whether programmes achieved outcomes or documented them elsewhere. The exact location test and the 5.7% coding rule still need confirmation: an IATI location need not identify a district, and results can represent outputs, outcomes, impacts or other measures. [5]

  • Reporting happens mostly at the end.

02Evidence+
WHAT OFTEN HAPPENS NOW
  • Teams reconstruct from documents, emails, meetings and memory.
  • Evidence is often detached from claims.
WHAT MTO MAKES POSSIBLE
  • The record builds as updates are produced.
  • Every approved item links back to its source.
03Results+
WHAT OFTEN HAPPENS NOW
  • Reports can focus on indicators and final results.
  • The reasons behind results can be difficult to recover.
WHAT MTO MAKES POSSIBLE
  • Decisions, changes, risks, adaptations and outcomes stay connected.
  • The pathway behind the result remains visible.
04Learning+
WHAT OFTEN HAPPENS NOW
  • Teams depend on people remembering what mattered months later.
  • Lessons are often recorded only at the end.
WHAT MTO MAKES POSSIBLE
  • Evidence and context are preserved while they are fresh.
  • Learning is captured during implementation and can inform future programmes.
