White Paper / Understand MatOc

Overview

What Map the Outcome is, how it works and the impact it is built to test.

Current statusMatOc is in development. This paper describes the proposed product, its workflow and what pilots need to establish.

Map the Outcome (MatOc) is a programme intelligence system in development. It organises the documentation teams already produce into a reviewed, source-linked record of implementation and outcomes.

Teams contribute updates, field reports, meeting notes and other programme documents. AI proposes relevant evidence, decisions, changes, risks and lessons. People check those proposals before they enter the programme record. Approved items remain connected to their original sources and to the outcomes they help explain.

The intended impact is less repeated evidence assembly, clearer reporting and a programme history that remains useful through staff changes and future funding cycles. These benefits are a pilot hypothesis, not proven product results.

What problem does it address?

The information needed to explain a programme can be spread across folders, reports, emails and people's memories. Finding a document is only one part of the work. A reviewer also needs to understand what the document supports, which decision it informed and how it relates to the intended outcome. MatOc is designed to preserve those relationships while implementation continues.

How does it work?

The proposed workflow has five stages: contribute a source, extract candidate evidence, review the proposal, connect the approved item and use the resulting record. The initial focus is a manageable capture and review process for one programme. Integrations and cross-programme learning depend on later development and pilot findings.

The system helps organise People remain responsible for
Source material and candidate evidence Deciding what a source actually supports
Connections between changes, risks and results Interpreting uncertainty and alternative explanations
A record for reporting and evaluation Writing, validating and assessing the final account

What is MatOc not?

MatOc is not a replacement for monitoring systems, independent evaluation or project management. A source link does not establish causality. The system's role is to make the evidence and its limitations easier to inspect, so people can ask better questions and make informed judgements.
