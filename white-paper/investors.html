Investors and partners

The proposed value case, pilot partnerships and commercial questions still to be tested.

MatOc's proposed value lies in making programme evidence reusable across reporting, evaluation preparation, handover and learning. The commercial question is whether that value is important enough for organisations to adopt and sustain the system alongside their existing tools.

What partners can help establish

Programme partners can provide a realistic workflow, an authorised source collection and staff feedback. M&E specialists can help define evidence quality and appropriate review. Evaluators can test whether the resulting record improves their starting point. These are possible partnership roles, not a list of confirmed partners.

What an investor should look for

The early milestones are a functioning review process, repeat use by a programme team and a credible account of total effort and usefulness. Adoption matters as much as extraction accuracy. A technically impressive prototype has limited value if staff cannot keep the record current or trust its source links.

Commercial questions remain open

Pricing, procurement requirements, support needs and the eventual revenue model are still to be tested. No market-size estimate, committed investment or revenue claim is made here. The first value case should be grounded in observed workflows and a clear comparison with the organisation's existing approach.

Dependencies to examine

The proposition depends on access to useful source material, human review capacity, permission controls and the ability to integrate with organisational practice. It also depends on whether a programme actually uses the record for decisions and reporting.

The next conversation

The immediate invitation is to discuss a bounded pilot, research collaboration, product feedback or development support. A useful first conversation identifies the programme, the reporting problem, who would use the record and how both sides would assess whether it helped.
