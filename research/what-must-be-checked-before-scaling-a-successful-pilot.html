What must be checked before scaling a successful pilot?

Success in one setting opens a new question about the next.

MMap the OutcomeEditorial desk · Updated 9 September 2026

Key takeaway

  • A pilot result needs its conditions and delivery context.
  • Scaling changes the people, resources and constraints involved.
  • A programme record should make those differences inspectable.

A pilot result gives a programme designer evidence to consider. Moving into a new setting requires an additional judgement about which parts of that evidence will still apply.

Eva Vivalt's research on generalising impact evaluations finds substantial variation in effects and systematic differences associated with study characteristics. The analysis helps explain why an estimate should travel with information about how and where it was generated. It does not provide a rule that predicts the outcome of every future implementation. Vivalt, Journal of the European Economic Association, 2020

For an organisation considering expansion, I would want the pilot record to explain more than the headline result. What support did the delivery team receive? Which participants were included? What resources were available? What changes were made during implementation? Which conditions might be difficult to reproduce?

Imagine a service model tested by a small team with frequent specialist support. A larger programme may use different staffing arrangements or operate in more varied locations. Those differences are questions to investigate, not automatic reasons to abandon expansion.

MatOc's proposed programme network should make these conditions searchable alongside outcomes. A useful match between programmes would explain why the earlier experience is relevant and where the comparison becomes uncertain.

I would want the next team to inherit enough context to design its own test. That is a stronger basis for expansion than treating a successful result as a complete implementation recipe. Programme memory becomes valuable when it helps people identify what needs to be preserved, adapted and checked again.
