What else has to work for an intervention to succeed?

The headline activity may be only one part of the explanation.

MMap the OutcomeEditorial desk · Updated 9 September 2026

Key takeaway

  • An intervention depends on delivery conditions and supporting work.
  • A useful description retains the parts needed to understand implementation.
  • Future users need to see what the headline leaves out.

Imagine describing a livelihoods programme as an asset transfer. That label might leave out training, temporary consumption support, coaching and the practical arrangements that helped participants use the asset.

Banerjee and colleagues evaluated an integrated approach to improving livelihoods in six randomized trials. The package combined several forms of support, and the study reported benefits extending beyond implementation. Because the package was evaluated together, its findings do not establish that any single component would produce the same result alone. Science, 2015

This matters when organisations try to learn from earlier programmes. A concise description can be useful for navigation, but the underlying record needs enough detail to identify what was actually delivered.

Which elements were included? How were they sequenced? What support accompanied the main activity? Which elements changed? What resources and relationships were needed to deliver them?

I would want a programme record to preserve those details where they help explain the result. The next team should be able to distinguish the broad intervention category from the version tested in a particular place.

That is also a useful discipline for MatOc's future network. Programmes should not be treated as comparable simply because their documents share a label. A connection should help a reader inspect relevant similarities and differences.

The practical ambition is modest but important: let someone identify which parts of an earlier experience they intend to reproduce and which they are changing. That creates a clearer basis for learning from what happens next.
