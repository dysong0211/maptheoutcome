What happens between delivery and the outcome?

Activities happened. What changed for the people they were meant to reach?

MMap the OutcomeEditorial desk · Updated 9 September 2026

Key takeaway

  • Delivery, participation and outcomes answer different questions.
  • A reported signal should retain its source and uncertainty.
  • Connected records can guide investigation without proving causality.

A programme can deliver its planned activities and still need to explain whether those activities changed anything meaningful. That explanation requires a record of what followed delivery, what else influenced events and where the original assumptions held or failed.

MOPAN's 2026 assessment of UNDP illustrates this distinction: strong output-level performance coexisted with a corporate framework that could not systematically assess longer-term outcomes and impacts. Its WFP assessment also identified weaknesses in using monitoring for learning and adjustment. MOPAN, UNDP, MOPAN, WFP

For an illustrative maternal-health programme, the number of outreach sessions answers one question. Attendance answers another. Whether women received appropriate care, who remained excluded and why participation changed require further evidence.

MatOc should help organise the pathway between these questions. A schedule change, stock shortage, community observation and attendance record can be linked without pretending they establish a complete causal explanation.

The distinction between a signal and a verified finding is essential. If a field note suggests that Saturday sessions improved attendance, the record should preserve that suggestion, identify its source and show what needs checking. An evaluator may later find supporting evidence, competing explanations or a different result.

This gives the programme team a more useful starting point during implementation as well as at evaluation. The question becomes specific: which part of the pathway needs investigation, and which evidence would help? MatOc's value depends on whether it makes that inquiry easier.
