When does evidence become useful to a decision?

A finding earns attention when someone can use it.

MMap the OutcomeEditorial desk · Updated 9 September 2026

Key takeaway

  • Evidence availability and evidence use are different questions.
  • Timing and responsibility affect whether information can inform action.
  • The programme record should retain how evidence shaped a choice.

Publishing a finding creates an opportunity for it to be used. Whether it reaches a person facing a relevant decision is another question.

Hjort and colleagues conducted experiments involving 2,150 Brazilian municipalities. In one experiment, providing mayors with research about taxpayer reminder letters increased adoption of that policy by ten percentage points. The study demonstrates an effect for a particular information intervention and policy decision, not a universal conversion rate from research to action. American Economic Review, 2021

For a programme evidence system, I would start with the decisions users need to make. A programme lead may need to choose between delivery approaches. A partner may need to understand why a timeline changed. An evaluator may need to assess competing explanations for a result.

Those are different information needs. A useful record should help each reader reach the relevant evidence without disguising the uncertainty around it.

Imagine a team reconsidering a delivery model. The most useful view might bring together the original assumption, implementation experience, participant feedback, costs and the options still available. A large collection of loosely related documents would require another layer of work before it could serve that decision.

MatOc should be designed around these moments of use. Its value would become clearer if a team could explain which information it consulted, how that information affected its judgement and what it decided to investigate next.

For a pilot, that suggests looking beyond records created or searches completed. Ask whether the evidence reached a real decision and whether the user could understand its relevance and limits.
