How does a lesson reach the next decision?

“Engage stakeholders earlier” is advice. What makes it usable?

MMap the OutcomeEditorial desk · Updated 9 September 2026

Key takeaway

  • A transferable lesson needs context, evidence and limits.
  • Similar wording is a weak basis for moving advice across programmes.
  • The useful test is whether the lesson informs a real choice.

“Engage stakeholders earlier” may be sensible advice. Without context, it gives the next programme little help deciding whom to involve, when to involve them or what problem the advice is meant to prevent.

A useful lesson should carry enough information to be judged. What happened? Under which conditions? What did the team change? What evidence suggests the change helped? Where might the lesson fail to apply?

GEF's recent knowledge-management assessment identifies continuing weaknesses in capturing and sharing lessons despite formal improvements. MOPAN also found barriers to moving useful WFP knowledge across countries. These findings support attention to how knowledge reaches decisions, as well as how it is stored. GEF OPS8, knowledge management, MOPAN, WFP 2024

For MatOc, this suggests a demanding definition of a lesson. A label attached to a paragraph is only the beginning. The record should connect the lesson to an intervention, a setting, an observed result and the source behind it.

A future network could then help a programme designer find relevant experience across programmes. Relevance would need to reflect meaningful conditions, including who participated, delivery constraints and differences between locations. Similar wording alone would be a weak basis for transferring advice.

This is a development ambition to test, not an established MatOc capability. Early pilots should ask whether another team actually used a recorded lesson and whether it informed a concrete design choice.

Counting lessons stored would tell us how much material accumulated. Documenting how a lesson informed the next decision would tell us whether the learning system became useful.
