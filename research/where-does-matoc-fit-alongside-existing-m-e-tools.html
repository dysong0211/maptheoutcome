Where does MatOc fit alongside existing M&E tools?

Another system is only useful if it improves the combined workflow.

MMap the OutcomeEditorial desk · Updated 9 September 2026

Key takeaway

  • Established tools already support data collection and evaluation tasks.
  • MatOc’s proposed entry point is existing implementation documentation.
  • A pilot must show where a combined workflow is worthwhile.

MatOc will enter an existing ecosystem of programme data and evaluation tools. Its purpose needs to be clear to an organisation that already has systems, trained staff and reporting obligations.

Public product descriptions show substantial capabilities already available. ActivityInfo supports programme data collection and reporting. DevResults supports monitoring and results management. TolaData offers M&E workflows and describes an AI development roadmap. UNDP's evaluation office also provides access to AI-assisted evaluation search through AIDA. These descriptions establish available approaches and announced plans; they do not provide a comparative performance test. ActivityInfo, DevResults, TolaData AI, UNDP evaluation office

MatOc's proposed entry point is the documentation teams already produce during implementation. The product would propose outcome-relevant records, ask people to review them and retain their connections to sources, changes, risks and intended outcomes.

That focus needs to translate into a practical advantage. Can a team add a source once and reuse its reviewed evidence? Can an evaluator understand why an approach changed? Can a successor find the context behind an unresolved risk?

Integration is part of that test. MatOc should respect existing permissions, preserve useful exports and work alongside established results frameworks. If it requires users to maintain another disconnected version of the programme, its proposed benefit becomes harder to realise.

The strongest positioning will come from demonstrated performance for a defined group of users. A pilot should establish which tasks MatOc improves, what existing tools already do well and where a combined workflow is worthwhile. That would give the product a clearer foundation for adoption and future development.
